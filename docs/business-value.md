# Industry Relevance and Business Value

Potential uses of the design include:

- government land registry integration
- property management platforms
- real-estate technology and proptech
- housing society records
- mortgage/loan verification workflows
- property due-diligence systems
- title verification
- document authentication

Potential business value:

- transparent ownership records
- tamper-evident history
- faster verification workflows
- reduced duplicate records
- easier ownership tracking
- improved auditability
- digital transfer workflows

The key qualification is that production value depends on legal recognition, trusted data entry, identity verification, and integration with authoritative systems.
